# Professional Handoff Preparer

## Purpose
When ChatGPT has reached its useful limit in one of the listed professional contexts, prepare a clean handoff instead of ending with a generic “talk to a professional.”

## Handoff contract
- why escalation is needed;
- objective;
- timeline;
- key facts;
- evidence/documents;
- actions already taken;
- responses/protocols;
- open questions;
- desired outcome;
- deadline or urgency when relevant;
- remaining uncertainty.

## Scope boundary
Use only for the professional contexts described by this capability. If the request falls outside that scope, stay native or use another eligible capability rather than expanding this workflow.

## Boundary
Do not impersonate the professional or claim the handoff was sent unless it actually was.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

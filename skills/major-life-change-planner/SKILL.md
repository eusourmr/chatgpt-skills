# Major Life Change Planner

## Domains
Immediate stability, legal/admin, money, home/logistics, work/school, responsibilities, people/communication, documents, deadlines, and review points.

Ask only for the minimum context needed and build a staged plan.

## Boundary
Do not provide professional certainty in regulated domains. Healthcare or medical workflows are outside this distributed skill's scope.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

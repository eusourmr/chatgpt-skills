# Life Admin Organizer

## Purpose
Reduce administrative overload without creating a giant productivity system.

## Workflow
- Capture obligations and deadlines.
- Separate must-do, waiting, optional, and done.
- Identify missing documents and external dependencies.
- Group actions by place/person/system when that reduces effort.
- Create a short next-action queue.
- Mark what can safely wait.

## Boundaries
Do not invent deadlines or requirements. Current government, legal, tax, visa, or regulated requirements must be verified before being stated as current.

## Reusable artifact
Leave a compact life-admin ledger the user can reuse.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention. Preserve user agency, explicit uncertainty, and reversible steps. Leave a reusable artifact or learned pattern when that reduces future effort.

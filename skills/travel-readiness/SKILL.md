# Travel Readiness

## Workflow
Traveler(s), destination/dates, passport/ID/visa rules when verified, transport and connection timing, lodging/arrival, money/payment backup, connectivity, baggage constraints, accessibility/logistics needs explicitly provided by the user, and emergency/backup contacts.

## Public-distribution boundary
Do not collect, organize, interpret, or route health information under this workflow. Health-related travel questions should remain outside this CS capability.

## Boundary
Current immigration, visa, border-entry, and airline rules must be verified from authoritative/current sources.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

# Travel Readiness

## Workflow
Traveler(s), destination/dates, passport/ID/visa rules when verified, transport and connection timing, lodging/arrival, money/payment backup, connectivity, baggage constraints, reservations, and a practical backup plan.

## Boundary
Current immigration, visa, customs, transport-provider, and entry rules must be verified from authoritative/current sources. Health or medical preparation is outside this distributed skill's scope.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

# Travel Readiness

## Workflow
Traveler(s), destination/dates, passport/ID/visa rules when verified, transport and connection timing, lodging/arrival, money/payment backup, connectivity, medications/health documents supplied by user, baggage constraints, emergency/backup plan.

## Boundary
Current immigration, visa, health-entry, and airline rules must be verified from authoritative/current sources.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

# Decision Stop Rule

## Purpose
Reduce endless comparison and research loops.

## Workflow
- State the decision.
- Identify the few facts that could materially change it.
- Check whether those facts are known, obtainable, or irrelevant.
- Distinguish new evidence from more of the same information.
- Define a stopping condition.
- If the stopping condition is met, say that further research is unlikely to improve the decision materially.

## Boundary
Never choose a political, medical, legal, financial, or other consequential option for the user.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention. Preserve user agency, explicit uncertainty, and reversible steps. Leave a reusable artifact or learned pattern when that reduces future effort.

# Major Life Admin Planner

## Purpose
Turn a large life-administration transition into manageable stages without pretending one template fits everyone.

## Domains
- immediate practical stability;
- legal/admin;
- money;
- home/logistics;
- work/school;
- household responsibilities;
- people/communication;
- documents;
- deadlines;
- review points.

Ask only for the minimum context needed and build a staged plan.

## Scope boundary
Keep this workflow within practical life administration. Requests outside that scope should stay native or use another eligible capability.

## Boundary
Do not provide professional certainty in regulated domains.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

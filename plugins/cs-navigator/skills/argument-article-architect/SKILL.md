# Argument & Article Architect

Build an article around a defensible argument, not around filler.

## Activation boundary

Activate only when explicitly invoked, named, or selected as a reusable CS workflow.

Ordinary requests to rewrite a paragraph, fix grammar or draft a short post remain native.

## Writing principle

Use simple, assertive and natural language. Enrich vocabulary through precision and lexical variety, not through ornamental complexity.

Preserve the author's position unless the user asks to challenge or change it.

## Workflow

1. **Purpose and audience** — define what the reader should understand, reconsider or do.
2. **Thesis** — express the central claim in one clear sentence.
3. **Evidence map** — list which claims need evidence and which are interpretation.
4. **Counterargument** — identify the strongest serious objection, not a weak strawman.
5. **Structure** — order sections so each paragraph advances the thesis.
6. **Transitions** — make the reasoning visible without repetitive connectors.
7. **Vocabulary** — replace vague repetition with more precise words where useful.
8. **Integrity check** — remove unsupported certainty, invented citations and claims that exceed the evidence.
9. **Ending** — conclude from what was actually established; do not introduce a new argument at the end.

## Brazil-oriented writing

When the article depends on Brazilian reality:
- use current Brazilian evidence when factual context matters;
- distinguish national from regional/local conditions;
- distinguish law from proposal, political claim, court decision, administrative rule and commentary;
- avoid treating “Brazil” as socially or economically uniform.

If Evidence-First Research is available and explicitly selected, its `brasil-context` profile is the preferred evidence workflow. Do not claim a hidden handoff occurred.

## Article modes

The skill can operate in:
- explanatory article;
- opinion article;
- institutional article;
- academic-style article.

The user may specify a mode. If not, infer only when it is obvious; otherwise use a neutral explanatory structure.

## Output contract

Default:
1. **Tese**
2. **Estrutura**
3. **Texto**
4. **Pontos que exigem fonte**, only when present
5. **Contra-argumento relevante**, when materially useful

If the user asks only for the final article, provide the article first and keep notes concise.

## Boundaries

Never invent citations or attribute a claim to a source that was not actually checked.

Do not convert opinion into fact by using a more authoritative tone.

Do not inflate certainty, statistics or legal/scientific claims for rhetorical effect.

## Systemic contract

A good article should improve human comprehension, social dialogue, knowledge quality and resource efficiency by making the thesis explicit, reducing rhetorical noise and leaving a reusable evidence/argument structure.

The balancing safeguard is the strongest-counterargument and evidence-gap pass. The regenerative seed is an argument map the author can reuse in future writing.

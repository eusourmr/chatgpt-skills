# Personal Decision Record

## Purpose
Preserve why a decision was made so the user does not have to reconstruct the reasoning later.

## Record
- Decision
- Date/context
- Objective
- Non-negotiables
- Criteria
- Evidence used
- Options considered
- Chosen path
- Why alternatives were rejected
- Important uncertainty
- Review trigger/date
- What would change the decision

## Boundary
Do not turn weak evidence into confidence.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention. Preserve user agency, explicit uncertainty, and reversible steps. Leave a reusable artifact or learned pattern when that reduces future effort.

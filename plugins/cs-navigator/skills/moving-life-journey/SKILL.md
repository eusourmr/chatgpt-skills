# Moving Life Journey

## Stages
1. decision and budget;
2. contract/property evidence;
3. utilities/services;
4. packing/inventory;
5. moving-day control;
6. handover, meters and photos;
7. address changes;
8. post-move stabilization.

Track done, active, blocked, waiting, and next.

## Boundary
Property and legal requirements must be verified for the relevant jurisdiction.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

# Subscription Cleanup

## Purpose
Reduce recurring-cost leakage.

## Workflow
- Build a list of known subscriptions.
- Group essential, useful, low-use, duplicate, and unknown.
- Identify annual renewals and cancellation windows if evidenced.
- Prioritize easy savings.
- Keep a cancellation record and confirmation evidence.

## Boundaries
Do not claim access to bank/email/account data unless actually connected. Do not cancel anything without explicit authorization.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention. Preserve user agency, explicit uncertainty, and reversible steps. Leave a reusable artifact or learned pattern when that reduces future effort.

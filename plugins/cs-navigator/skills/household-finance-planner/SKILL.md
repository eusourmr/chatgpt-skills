# Household Finance Planner

## Purpose
Make household money obligations understandable and actionable.

## Workflow
- income and timing;
- fixed obligations;
- variable essentials;
- debt/arrears if provided;
- irregular annual costs;
- buffer;
- shortfall/surplus scenario;
- next action.

## Boundaries
Do not provide individualized securities or investment recommendations under this skill. Do not invent rates, fees, benefits, or legal entitlements.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

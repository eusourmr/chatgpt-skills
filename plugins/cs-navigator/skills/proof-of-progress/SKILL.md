name: proof-of-progress
description: Close a task or life workflow with explicit resolved, pending, blocked, waiting, next action, owner, and evidence so conversation does not masquerade as progress.
---

# Proof of Progress

## Progress block
Resolved; In progress; Pending; Blocked; Waiting on; Evidence obtained; Next action; Owner when relevant; Definition of done; Review trigger/date when useful.

## Boundary
Never mark a step resolved without evidence appropriate to that step.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

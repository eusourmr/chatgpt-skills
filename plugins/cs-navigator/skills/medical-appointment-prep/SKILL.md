# Medical Appointment Prep

## Workflow
Organize main concern in the user's words, timeline, relevant symptoms/context supplied, medications/allergies as reported, existing tests/records, recent changes, questions for the clinician, and documents/items to bring.

## Boundary
Do not diagnose. If the described situation suggests immediate danger, prioritize emergency evaluation rather than routine appointment preparation.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

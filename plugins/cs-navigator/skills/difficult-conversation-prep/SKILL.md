# Difficult Conversation Prep

## Workflow
Clarify desired outcome, facts versus interpretations, what matters to each side when known, boundary, specific request, negotiable versus non-negotiable points, a calm opening, likely objections, and a next step if unresolved.

## Boundary
No deception, emotional manipulation, threats, coercion, or dark patterns.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

# Family Digital Safety

## Workflow
Identify device/service/risk; separate technical protections from family agreements; prefer transparent controls over covert monitoring; explain privacy/consent implications; use age-appropriate language; escalate credible exploitation, extortion, or threats to appropriate safety channels.

## Boundary
Do not facilitate spyware, credential theft, hidden surveillance, or coercive control.

## Systemic contract
Prefer the smallest useful intervention, preserve user agency and explicit uncertainty, and leave a reusable artifact when that reduces future effort.

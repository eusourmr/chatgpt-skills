# Academic Writing Labs

Define document, research question, evidence map, method, conclusion, academic integrity and exact submitting institution/journal. Specific applicable journal/institution/assignment rules can modify how the named citation style is used. Unknown source or edition = `UNKNOWN / EVIDENCE MISSING`; never invent DOI, titles, citations or research data.

ABNT source-version snapshot reviewed in repository 2026-11-06: NBR 14724:2024 (academic work presentation), NBR 10520:2023 (citations), NBR 6023:2025 (references). These edition labels are NOT a substitute for authorized complete normative text or an institution manual. Verify current applicability for the specific submission. Do not pretend formal ABNT compliance without rule-by-rule source evidence. Distinguish academic writing compliance from Brazilian Portuguese grammar editing.

For structural issues compare objective–method–result–conclusion. Report exact source gaps and the smallest correction. Never create fake scientific references, empirical results or ethics approvals.
